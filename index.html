Mission

Health Records for Everyone (HR4E) is a 501(c)(3) organization founded in 2010 to make longitudinal, portable electronic health records available to every person, regardless of geography or income.

hr4e-revisited is the 2026 relaunch of that vision, focused on practical tools: vaults, editors, extractors, and billing flows that can be deployed in clinics, schools, and relief settings.

Foundation architecture

The hr4e-revisited foundation layer includes:

  • Vaults: portable, USB-friendly stores of patient documents and FHIR-like data.
  • Studio: a web-based environment for viewing, editing, and aggregating vaults.
  • Extract/Load: tools to populate vaults from existing EHRs (Epic and others).
  • Billing & reporting: low-overhead tools for small clinics and franchisees.

Technical documentation and open-source repos are hosted under the hr4e-revisited GitHub organization.

Projects and pilots

  • Rural school health assessment in developing countries.
  • Small clinics in resource-limited settings.
  • Ireland–Northern Ireland interoperability for Epic-based sites.

As pilots mature, each will have its own page describing context, partners, and technical flows.

Contribute

Developers, clinicians, and organizations can participate by:

  • Contributing to open-source repos under hr4e-revisited.
  • Proposing new vault profiles or deployment scenarios.
  • Collaborating on pilots and research projects.

Formal contribution guidelines and governance documents will be published here as the foundation evolves.
