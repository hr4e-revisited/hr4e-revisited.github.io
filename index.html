Mission

Health Records for Everyone (HR4E) was founded in 2010 as a 501(c)(3) charitable organization to make longitudinal, portable electronic health records available to every person, regardless of geography or income.

hr4e-revisited is the 2026 relaunch of that vision, focused on practical tools: vaults, editors, extractors, and billing tools that can be deployed in clinics, schools, and relief settings. We are actively working on re-establishing the organization; meanwhile we are moving ahead with development of these tools.

HR4E vaults also support personal electronic health passports (eHPs), a modern evolution of paper health passports long used in by patients overseas as they get care by different providers in different places. With today’s widespread mobile devices and secure biometric authentication, eHPs finally make it practical for patients to carry their own portable, offline-first health records between encounters while keeping a synchronized backup whenever connectivity is available.

Foundation architecture

The hr4e-revisited foundation layer includes:

  • Vaults: portable, USB-friendly stores of HL7 CCD-format clinical documents.
  • Editor/Viewer: platform-specific apps for editing or viewing clinical documents from those vaults.
  • Extractor/Loader: tools to populate vaults from existing EHRs (Epic +/- others).
  • Biller/Bookkeeper: simple tools to submit/record and track billing transactions for services documented in vaults; to serve as the infrastructure for billing operations and franchises.
  • Analyzer/Reporter: low-overhead reporting tools for small clinics and franchisees against data recorded in vaults.
  • Studio: a configurable web-based environment to access those tools with or without Internet access.

Technical documentation and open-source repos are hosted under the hr4e-revisited GitHub organization.

Projects and pilots

  • Rural school health assessment in developing countries.
  • Small +/- mobile clinics in resource-limited settings.
  • Efforts supporting care between non-Epic and Epic-based sites.

As pilots mature, each will have its own page describing context, partners, and technical flows.

Contribute

Developers, clinicians, and organizations can participate by:

  • Contributing to open-source repos under hr4e-revisited.
  • Proposing new vault profiles or deployment scenarios.
  • Collaborating on pilots and research projects.

Formal contribution guidelines and governance documents will be published here as the foundation evolves.
